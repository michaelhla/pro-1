

# Carbonic Anhydrase Stability Engineering Summary

## Project Objective
Design stable carbonic anhydrase variants while maintaining catalytic function.

## Design Approaches and Results

### Initial Conservative Design (CA_RationalStable)
- **Mutations**: V143C and V207I
- **Rosetta Score**: -288.16 REU (most stable of all designs)
- **RMSD vs Reference**: 0.143 Å (excellent native fold preservation)
- **Catalytic Integrity**: Excellent (active site RMSD: 0.060 Å)
- **Secondary Structure**: 18.1% helix, 26.5% sheet, 55.4% loop

### Second Conservative Design (CA_OptimizedStable)
- **Mutations**: D139E, V143C, and V207I
- **Rosetta Score**: -284.94 REU (slightly less stable than CA_RationalStable)
- **RMSD vs Reference**: 0.148 Å (excellent native fold preservation)
- **Catalytic Integrity**: Excellent (active site RMSD: 0.061 Å)

### Bold Designs with Major Structural Modifications
- **CA_CoilStable** (coiled-coil motifs)
  - **Rosetta Score**: -251.54 REU
  - **RMSD vs Reference**: 20.586 Å (significant structural changes)
  - **Catalytic Integrity**: Well preserved despite global fold changes

- **CA_ZincFingerInsert** (zinc finger domain insertion)
  - **Rosetta Score**: 210.65 REU (positive/unfavorable energy)
  - **Catalytic Integrity**: Excellent (active site RMSD: 0.092 Å)
  - Most promising of the bold designs, but still unstable

- **Other Bold Designs Attempted**:
  - CA_BetaPropeller_ZincStabilized (Rosetta: 2653.01 REU)
  - CA_CompactZincStabilized (Rosetta: 1073.24 REU)
  - CA_ThermostableCoilInsert (Rosetta: 242.87 REU)
  - CA_CoilTail_ZincFinger (Rosetta: 1772.34 REU)
  - CA_PfMBP_Fusion (Rosetta: 5104.28 REU)
  - CA_Thermostable_ZincFinger (Rosetta: 433.57 REU)
  - CA_CTerminal_ZincFinger (Rosetta: 316.61 REU)

## Key Findings
1. Conservative point mutations (V143C, V207I) yielded the most stable structures while perfectly preserving the native fold and catalytic function
2. Major structural modifications (domain insertions, fusion proteins) generally resulted in unfavorable energy scores
3. The zinc finger domain insertion showed the most promise among bold designs, preserving catalytic function but with unfavorable energy
4. All designs maintained excellent catalytic site geometry (RMSD < 0.1 Å) despite global fold differences

## Current Status
The CA_RationalStable variant with V143C and V207I mutations remains the most promising design, offering improved stability while maintaining native structure and function.
