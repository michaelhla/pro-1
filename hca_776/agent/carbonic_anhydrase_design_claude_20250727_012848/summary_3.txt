# Carbonic Anhydrase Engineering Summary

## Main Objective
Design a hyperthermostable carbonic anhydrase variant by implementing major structural modifications rather than simple point mutations.

## Design Strategies Explored
1. **CA-HyperStable_v1**: Attempted calcium-binding EF-hand domain insertion with circular permutation
   - Sequence: Added "LDDKDGDGTITTKE" (calcium-binding motif) between positions 140-146 with GSGGG linker
   - Rosetta score: 3147.77 REU (very poor)
   - RMSD to original hCA2: 9.254 Å
   - Active site RMSD: 1.710 Å; Zinc binding RMSD: 0.052 Å

2. **CA-HyperStable_v2**: More conservative calcium-binding domain approach
   - Sequence: Added "DDKDGDGTITTK" calcium-binding motif at position 124
   - Rosetta score: -97.24 REU (much improved)
   - RMSD to original: 12.127 Å
   - Active site RMSD: 0.125 Å; Zinc binding RMSD: 10.116 Å (problematic)

3. **CA-HyperStable_v3**: Calcium-binding domain near C-terminus
   - Sequence: Added "DDKDGDGTITTK" near C-terminus position 199
   - Rosetta score: 497.44 REU
   - RMSD to original: 7.984 Å
   - Active site RMSD: 0.153 Å; Zinc binding RMSD: 0.064 Å (excellent)
   - Secondary structure: 14.7% helix, 25.0% sheet, 60.3% loop

4. **CA-ThermoZinc_v1** (in progress): Implementing strategies from thermophilic zinc metalloproteases
   - Key modifications:
     * H94C mutation (replacing histidine with cysteine for stronger zinc coordination)
     * H119C mutation (further strengthening zinc binding)
     * Adding thermostable zinc finger motif from Thermus thermophilus
     * Introducing disulfide bonds (N62C, H64C)
     * Protein folding completed but analysis pending

## Key Findings
1. Version 3 (CA-HyperStable_v3) showed the most promising results with:
   - Good preservation of both active site and zinc binding geometry
   - Compact structure (radius of gyration: 17.76 Å)
   - Good secondary structure content

2. Literature search revealed:
   - Thermophilic zinc metalloproteases often use cysteine instead of histidine for zinc coordination
   - Domain insertion and disulfide networks can enhance thermostability
   - Circular permutation strategies depend on distance from active sites
