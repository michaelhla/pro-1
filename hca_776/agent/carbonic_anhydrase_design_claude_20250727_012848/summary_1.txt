# Protein Engineering Project Summary: Designing Stable Carbonic Anhydrase Variants

## Main Objective
Design more stable variants of carbonic anhydrase (CA) for carbon capture applications that:
- Increase thermal stability
- Improve stability in acidic conditions
- Maintain catalytic activity

## Baseline Assessment
Original CA (sequence: MSHHWGYGKHNGPEHWHKDFPIAKGERQSPVDIDTHTAKYDPSLKPLSVSYDQATSLRILNNGHAFNVEFDDSQDKAVLKGGPLDGTYRLIQFHFHWGSLDGQGSEHTVDKKKYAAELHLVHWNTKYGDFGKAVQQPDGLAVLGIFLKVGSAKPGLQKVVDVLDSIKTKGKSADFTNFDPRGLLPESLDYWTYPGSLTTPPLLECVTWIVLKEPISVSSEQVLKFRKLNFNGEGEPEELMVDNWRPAQPLKNRQIKASFK):
- Rosetta score: -305.57 REU
- 260 residues (17.3% helix, 26.5% sheet, 56.2% loop)
- Critical functional residues: catalytic residues (Y7, N62, H64, N67, Q92) and zinc-binding residues (H94, H96, H119)

## Design Iterations

### Design 1: CA-ThermX
- Strategy: N-terminal fusion with Pyrococcus furiosus thioredoxin domain, proline-rich insert, metal-binding motif
- Results:
  - Rosetta score: 1944.79 REU (unfavorable)
  - Active site RMSD: 7.79 Å (poor catalytic preservation)
  - Zinc binding RMSD: 11.26 Å (significant disruption)

### Design 2: CA-StableFold
- Strategy: C-terminal fusion with thermostable domain, flexible GGGGS linker
- Results:
  - Rosetta score: 873.46 REU (improved but still unfavorable)
  - Active site RMSD: 0.087 Å (excellent catalytic preservation)
  - Zinc binding RMSD: 0.063 Å (excellent structural integrity)
  - Overall RMSD to original: 0.281 Å (maintains original fold)

### Design 3: CA-ThermoStable
- Strategy: C-terminal fusion with P. furiosus thioredoxin, strategic proline insertions (PPGP at position 154-157), triple GGGGS linker
- Sequence: MSHHWGYGKHNGPEHWHKDFPIAKGERQSPVDIDTHTAKYDPSLKPLSVSYDQATSLRILNNGHAFNVEFDDSQDKAVLKGGPLDGTYRLIQFHFHWGSLDGQGSEHTVDKKKYAAELHLVHWNTKYGDFGKAVQQPDGLAVLGIFLKVGSAKPGLQKVVDVLDSIKTPPGPIKTKGKSADFTNFDPRGLLPESLDYWTYPGSLTTPPLLECVTWIVLKEPISVSSEQVLKFRKLNFNGEGEPEELMVDNWRPAQPLKNRQIKASFEGGGGSGGGGSGGGGSMKVEIKAEEPIIVGDPVLSNVKYGPQGIGGIRYCVFQDNEDTIVIVDAKNKDYPGDFDTTLEEMGIDAFIDVYFYPGAAKAGLLKEIVQIRVQDVREAEKVIGVEFDGLFNQEIIKYFKSGCPVTAIKFISDFFGKSEIKKKVLKDGKMDDIKMFISTQLKKLVPLIANGAFFKM
- Results:
  - Rosetta score: 1677.99 REU (still unfavorable)
  - Active site RMSD: 0.12 Å (excellent catalytic preservation)
  - Zinc binding RMSD: 0.072 Å (excellent preservation of zinc coordination)
  - 457 residues (29.5% helix, 17.3% sheet, 53.2% loop)

## Key Findings
1. C-terminal fusions better preserve catalytic function than N-terminal fusions
2. Flexible linkers (GGGGS) help maintain functional integrity between domains
3. Strategic proline insertions in loop regions can enhance rigidity without disrupting function
4. Current designs maintain catalytic site geometry but have unfavorable Rosetta scores

## Next Steps
Next design iterations should focus on:
1. Addressing the unfavorable Rosetta scores while maintaining catalytic integrity
2. Exploring mutations in zinc metalloproteases for transferable stability-enhancing modifications
3. Investigating circular permutation approaches
4. Testing different insertion positions for stabilizing motifs
