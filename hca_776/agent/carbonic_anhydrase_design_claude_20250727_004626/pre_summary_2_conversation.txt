# Carbonic Anhydrase Engineering for Enhanced Stability Summary

## Objective
Design a more stable variant of human carbonic anhydrase II (hCA II) for carbon capture applications, focusing on increased thermal stability and acid resistance while maintaining catalytic activity.

## Key Research Findings
- Thermostable CA variants can be engineered through:
  - Surface residue modifications (replacing hydrophobic with hydrophilic)
  - Disulfide bond engineering (strategically placed for rigidity)
  - Proline substitutions (enhancing local rigidity)
  - Loop rigidification (reducing flexible regions)
  - Salt bridge optimization (enhancing electrostatic networks)
- A known thermostable sextuple mutant (A65T, L100H, K154N, L224S, L240P, A248T) has been documented

## Baseline Measurements
- Original sequence Rosetta score: -301.26 REU
- Secondary structure: 17.3% helix, 26.5% sheet, 56.2% loop
- Catalytic residues verified with near-zero RMSD (active site RMSD: 4.72×10⁻⁷)

## Mutations Tested
Multiple design iterations were tested with various mutations including:
1. Design v1: G6A, H36D, A65C, A69C, L100H, A140E, K154C, V161C, G196A, L240P, A248T, Q249E (Rosetta: -98.42)
2. Design v2: G6A, H36D, L100H, A140E, T193P, L240P, A248T, Q249E (Rosetta: -217.40)
3. Design v3: A65T, L100H, K154N, L224S, L240P, A248T (known thermostable set) (Rosetta: -169.24)
4. Design v4: A65T, L100H, L155P, L240P, W209T, A248T (Rosetta: -162.47)
5. Design v5: W209R (Rosetta: -128.44)
6. Design v6: A134T, C205S, V207L (Rosetta: -286.89) - Best scoring design
7. Design v7: A65T, L100H, K154N, L224S, L240P, A248T, W209T (Rosetta: -158.65)

## Best Design Results
Design v6 with mutations A134T, C205S, V207L achieved a Rosetta score of -286.89 REU, which is closest to the wild-type score (-301.26 REU).

## Conclusions
1. The attempted disulfide bond designs significantly destabilized the protein structure
2. Conservative mutations at specific surface residues produced the best results
3. Direct implementation of the known sextuple mutant pattern did not yield favorable Rosetta scores in our model
4. The best design (v6) introduced more subtle changes that maintained overall fold stability

## Next Steps
1. Further refine design v6 with additional stabilizing mutations
2. Test more conservative approaches focused on surface charge optimization
3. Examine catalytic activity of the best design to ensure function is preserved
4. Consider combined approaches with both core and surface stabilizing mutations
