- **Tight Packing and Increased Burial Depth**: Thermostable proteins, particularly from hyperthermophiles like *P. furiosus*, display *greater burial of core residues* and *increased compactness* compared to their mesophilic counterparts, resulting in fewer pockets and more sphericity[1]. - **Ion Pair Networks and Stabilizing Interactions**: Enhanced thermostability in proteins from both *T. thermophilus* and *P. furiosus* is often associated with increased numbers of *ion pairs*, *hydrogen bonds*, and *hydrophobic interactions*, especially at subunit interfaces[2][5].
