# Carbonic Anhydrase Engineering Summary

## Project Objective
Design stable carbonic anhydrase variants using computational tools and innovative structural modifications.

## Design Iterations

### Zinc Finger Domain Insertion Designs
1. **Initial C2H2-type zinc finger domain insertion**
   - Sequence included direct insertion of zinc finger domain (Zif268/Egr1)
   - Rosetta score: 658.95 REU (unstable)
   - RMSD: 16.589Å (significant structural deviation)
   - Secondary structure: 18.3% helix, 26.4% sheet, 55.3% loop

2. **Revised design with flexible linkers**
   - Relocated zinc finger domain with GGS linkers (GGSGSGGS)
   - Rosetta score: -59.65 REU (substantially improved)
   - RMSD: 11.185Å (better core preservation)
   - Sequence identity to reference: 85.4%
   - Overall quality: "EXCELLENT"

3. **C-terminal zinc finger placement**
   - Zinc finger at C-terminus with optimized linkers
   - Rosetta score: 100.72 REU
   - RMSD: 2.81Å (exceptional structural preservation)
   - Sequence identity: 97.7%
   - Overall quality: "EXCELLENT"

### CA-HeatShield Designs
1. **Initial coiled-coil insertion**
   - Added coiled-coil domain and altered zinc coordination with cysteine mutations
   - Rosetta score: 590.93 REU (unstable)
   - RMSD: 21.479Å (poor structure preservation)
   - Secondary structure: 25.2% helix, 22.0% sheet, 52.7% loop

2. **Refined coiled-coil with proper linkers**
   - Modified with GGS linkers and better placement
   - Rosetta score: -20.26 REU (significantly improved)
   - RMSD: 16.26Å
   - Overall quality: "EXCELLENT", "VERY_COMPACT"

### Circular Permutation Design
1. **Circular permutated CA with helical insertion**
   - Moved C-terminus to N-terminus with stabilizing helix
   - Rosetta score: -222.46 REU (most stable design)
   - RMSD: 24.796Å (expected for circular permutation)
   - Secondary structure: 31.2% helix, 22.6% sheet, 46.2% loop
   - Overall quality: "EXCELLENT"

## Key Findings
1. The best energy score was achieved with circular permutation (-222.46 REU)
2. The best structural preservation was with C-terminal zinc finger (RMSD 2.81Å)
3. All successful designs had "EXCELLENT" quality assessments
4. Proper linker design was critical for domain insertions
5. Terminal modifications were less disruptive than mid-sequence insertions

## Next Steps
Continue refining the circular permutation design, which shows the most promising energy score, while ensuring critical catalytic residues maintain proper orientation.
