# Engineering Thermostable Carbonic Anhydrase II: Summary of Design Iterations

I've conducted several design iterations to enhance the thermostability of human Carbonic Anhydrase II (hCA2) while preserving its catalytic function. Below is a comprehensive analysis of the designs:

## Design 9: Salt Bridge Network Strategy

**Mutations introduced:**
- A65R and S67D: Creating a new salt bridge in a flexible region
- N178K and D180E: Creating a networked salt bridge
- G145A: Reducing local flexibility by introducing a methyl group

**Results:**
- Rosetta Energy Score: -62.25 REU
- RMSD with reference: 0.363 Å
- Catalytic integrity maintained (overall RMSD: 0.105 Å)
- Secondary structure well-preserved

While this design showed good structural integrity, the Rosetta energy score suggested room for improvement in stability.

## Design 10: Disulfide Bond Strategy

**Mutations introduced:**
- S101C: First cysteine for disulfide bond
- I137C: Second cysteine for disulfide bond
- V143P: Rigidifying a flexible loop

**Results:**
- Rosetta Energy Score: -299.66 REU (significantly better than Design 9)
- RMSD with reference: 0.112 Å
- Excellent catalytic integrity (overall RMSD: 0.049 Å)
- Slightly reduced SASA (12,076 Å² vs 12,266 Å² in Design 9)

The disulfide bond approach resulted in a substantially more stable structure according to the Rosetta energy score.

## Design 11: Combined Optimized Approach

**Mutations introduced:**
- G88E: Introducing negative charge to form salt bridge
- A191R: Introducing positive charge to form salt bridge with G88E
- S101C and I137C: Disulfide bond from Design 10
- A210R: Additional stabilizing charge
- Q242R: Creating a surface salt bridge network
- The additional charge pairs strategically placed to form networked interactions

**Results:**
- Rosetta Energy Score: -192.06 REU (between Designs 9 and 10)
- RMSD with reference: 0.355 Å
- Excellent catalytic integrity (overall RMSD: 0.053 Å)
- Slightly higher SASA (12,304 Å²) but good compactness (Radius of gyration: 17.2 Å)

## Conclusions and Recommendations

Based on our computational analyses, **Design 10** emerges as the most promising candidate for enhanced thermostability with:
1. The best Rosetta energy score (-299.66 REU)
2. Excellent preservation of structural features (RMSD: 0.112 Å)
3. Fully intact catalytic machinery (catalytic RMSD: 0.049 Å)
4. Optimal secondary structure composition and compactness

The introduced disulfide bond between positions 101 and 137 appears to provide significant stabilization without compromising the protein's structure or function. This strategic placement is away from the active site, yet contributes to the overall rigidity of the protein scaffold.

### Next Steps:

1. **Experimental Validation**: Test Design 10 experimentally for:
   - Thermal stability (melting temperature, Tm)
   - Catalytic activity at different temperatures
   - Long-term stability under various conditions

2. **Further Optimization**: Consider combining the disulfide bond from Design 10 with selected salt bridge mutations from Designs 9 and 11 for potentially additive stabilization effects.

3. **Alternative Disulfide Positions**: If needed, explore additional disulfide bond positions using computational modeling to identify optimal pairs.

This iterative design approach has yielded a promising thermostable variant of carbonic anhydrase II that maintains its critical catalytic machinery while enhancing overall stability.
