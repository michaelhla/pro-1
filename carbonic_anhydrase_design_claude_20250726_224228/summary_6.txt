

# Carbonic Anhydrase Thermostabilization Summary

## Project Objective
Design thermostable variants of human carbonic anhydrase II (hCA II) using novel stabilization strategies not previously applied to this enzyme.

## Reference Structure Analysis
- **Rosetta score**: -303.03 REU
- **Secondary structure**: 17.3% helix, 26.5% sheet, 56.2% loop
- **Surface hydrophobicity**: 48.6%
- **Key catalytic elements**: Zinc binding (H94, H96, H119) and proton transfer network (Y7, N62, H64, N67, Q92)

## Design 7: Metal Coordination and Core Packing Strategy
**Mutations**:
- K9Y: N-terminal π-π stacking
- L57F: Core packing optimization
- E117D: Metal coordination sphere tuning
- V121I: Hydrophobic packing improvement
- A134E: Salt bridge with R136
- T199I: Hydrophobic core strengthening
- P202D: Ionic interaction with K171
- M241W: C-terminal stabilization

**Results**:
- Rosetta score: -273.64 REU
- RMSD from reference: 0.293 Å
- Active site RMSD: 0.112 Å
- Zinc binding RMSD: 0.051 Å
- Secondary structure preserved (17.3% helix, 26.5% sheet, 56.2% loop)
- Surface hydrophobicity: 49.0%

## Design 8: Cavity-Filling and Entropy Reduction Strategy
**Mutations**:
- V135W, S217M: Cavity filling
- A85R: Electrostatic interactions in frustrated region
- L184P: Loop rigidification
- S197Y: Enhanced hydrophobic packing
- A65Y: Cation-π interactions
- T200A: Entropy reduction
- L120I: Core packing fine-tuning

**Results**:
- Rosetta score: -195.16 REU
- RMSD: 2.494 Å
- Zinc binding RMSD: 1.505 Å (too high)
- Secondary structure slightly altered (16.2% helix, 26.3% sheet, 57.5% loop)

## Design 9: Revised Approach with Catalytic Region Protection
**Mutations**:
- S220A, A170F, P201W, P198L, S192P (focus on preserving catalytic machinery)

**Results**:
- Rosetta score: -201.80 REU
- RMSD: 0.267 Å (excellent structural preservation)
- Surface hydrophobicity: 49.9% (improvement)
- Secondary structure preserved (17.3% helix, 26.5% sheet, 56.2% loop)

## Current Status
Design 7 shows the most promising balance between improved stability (better Rosetta score) and preserved function (low catalytic site RMSD). Design 9 maintains excellent structural integrity but with less energetic improvement.

## Next Steps
1. Combine successful mutations from Designs 7 and 9
2. Consider additional novel strategies:
   - Halogen bonding with non-canonical amino acids
   - Biospecific covalent cross-linking
   - Dynamic allostery optimization
3. Perform molecular dynamics simulations at elevated temperatures
4. Experimentally validate top designs through thermal denaturation experiments
