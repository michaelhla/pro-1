# Carbonic Anhydrase Engineering Project Summary

## Main Objective
Design more stable carbonic anhydrase variants while maintaining catalytic activity for carbon capture applications.

## Tested Design Strategies

### 1. Calcium Binding Site Engineering
- **Single Calcium Binding Site Variant (DADGDG at position 140-145)**
  - Rosetta score: -274.52 REU
  - Radius of gyration: 17.22 Å
  - Catalytic site integrity maintained (active site RMSD: 0.026 Å, zinc binding RMSD: 0.004 Å)
  - Secondary structure: 16.2% helix, 26.5% sheet, 57.3% loop

- **Dual Calcium Binding Site Variant (second site at position 190-195)**
  - Rosetta score: -179.43 REU 
  - Radius of gyration: 17.27 Å
  - Catalytic site integrity maintained (active site RMSD: 0.082 Å, zinc binding RMSD: 0.036 Å)
  - Secondary structure: 16.0% helix, 26.3% sheet, 57.6% loop

- **Refined Calcium Binding Variant (DxDxDG at position 222-227)**
  - Rosetta score: -272.14 REU
  - Most compact structure (radius of gyration: 17.17 Å)
  - Excellent catalytic site integrity (active site RMSD: 0.043 Å, zinc binding RMSD: 0.034 Å)
  - RMSD to wild-type: 1.815 Å
  - Secondary structure: 16.9% helix, 26.4% sheet, 56.7% loop

### 2. Secondary Structure Element Duplication
- **α-Helix Duplication (residues 22-35 duplicated after position 160)**
  - Rosetta score: 337.32 REU
  - Radius of gyration: 17.83 Å
  - Catalytic site integrity maintained (active site RMSD: 0.054 Å, zinc binding RMSD: 0.012 Å)
  - Secondary structure: 16.4% helix, 25.2% sheet, 58.4% loop

- **Circular Permutation (new termini at position 160)**
  - Rosetta score: 6511.10 REU (unfavorable)
  - Testing of β-strand duplication in progress

## Key Insights
- Calcium binding site engineering using DxDxDG motifs shows the most promising results
- The refined calcium binding variant (DxDxDG at position 222-227) offers the best combination of stability and catalytic integrity
- Secondary structure element duplication and circular permutation approaches need further optimization
