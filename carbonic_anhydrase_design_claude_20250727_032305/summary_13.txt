

# Carbonic Anhydrase Engineering Summary

## Project Objective
Design stable carbonic anhydrase variants using computational tools and innovative structural modifications.

## Design Iterations

### Zinc Finger Domain Designs
1. **Initial C2H2-type zinc finger domain insertion**
   - Direct insertion of Zif268/Egr1 zinc finger domain
   - Rosetta score: 658.95 REU (unstable)
   - RMSD: 16.589Å (significant deviation)

2. **Revised design with flexible linkers**
   - Zinc finger with GGS linkers (GGSGSGGS)
   - Rosetta score: -59.65 REU (improved)
   - RMSD: 11.185Å
   - Quality: "EXCELLENT"

3. **C-terminal zinc finger placement**
   - Zinc finger at C-terminus with optimized linkers
   - Rosetta score: 100.72 REU
   - RMSD: 2.81Å (excellent preservation)
   - Quality: "EXCELLENT"

### CA-HeatShield Designs
1. **Initial coiled-coil insertion**
   - Added coiled-coil domain with cysteine mutations
   - Rosetta score: 590.93 REU (unstable)
   - RMSD: 21.479Å (poor preservation)

2. **Refined coiled-coil with proper linkers**
   - Modified with GGS linkers
   - Rosetta score: -20.26 REU (improved)
   - RMSD: 16.26Å
   - Quality: "EXCELLENT"

### Circular Permutation Design
- Moved C-terminus to N-terminus with stabilizing helix
- Rosetta score: -222.46 REU (most stable of initial designs)
- RMSD: 24.796Å (expected for permutation)
- Quality: "EXCELLENT"

### CA-TrxZincFinger
- Thioredoxin domain fusion with zinc-binding motif
- Rosetta score: -81.69 REU
- High catalytic site RMSD (11.16Å)

### CA-ZincBindingMotif
- Added zinc-binding motif after residue 260
- Rosetta score: -108.79 REU
- RMSD: 9.271Å
- Quality: "GOOD", "VERY_COMPACT"

### CA-StrategicDisulfide
- Three disulfide bond pairs (C39-C47, C196-C206, C217-C223)
- Rosetta score: -259.35 REU (best score so far)
- RMSD: 1.962Å (excellent preservation)
- Catalytic site RMSD: 0.027Å (exceptional preservation)
- Quality: "GOOD", "VERY_COMPACT"
- Secondary structure: 17.2% helix, 26.4% sheet, 56.3% loop

## Key Findings
1. Best energy score: CA-StrategicDisulfide (-259.35 REU)
2. Best structural preservation: C-terminal zinc finger (2.81Å) and CA-StrategicDisulfide (1.962Å)
3. Proper linker design is critical for domain insertions
4. Strategic disulfide bonds provide stability with minimal structural disruption
5. Terminal modifications are less disruptive than mid-sequence insertions
