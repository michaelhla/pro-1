**Surface charge optimization** is an effective approach to improving **thermostability** of proteins, including carbonic anhydrase. - **Empirical and Computational Strategy:** 
    - **Experimental measurements** (such as determining pKa shifts between folded and unfolded states) help identify which residues to target for mutation or modification[1].
