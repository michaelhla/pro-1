The Hydrogen Bond Enhanced variant (Design 3) shows the most promise with the best Rosetta energy score (-315.51 REU) and excellent preservation of catalytic site geometry. This design builds on our successful salt bridge approach by introducing strategic glycine-to-alanine mutations that enhance backbone hydrogen bonding while maintaining the protein's overall structure.
