- Researchers have successfully **grafted disulfide bridges** from homologous, naturally stabilized proteins into HCA II[2][4]. ### Design Methodology

Most successful efforts use the following workflow:
1. **Structural analysis** (e.g., using crystal structure or homology models) to identify potential bond sites that are:
   - Spatially close (typically 4–7 Å apart in Cα atoms)
   - Not critical for catalytic activity or substrate binding[1][4]

3. **Experimental validation** by site-directed mutagenesis (introducing cysteines), expression, and thermostability assays[1][4][5].

- **Disulfide bonds** increase thermostability by restricting local and global flexibility, thus raising the activation energy required for unfolding[1][5].
