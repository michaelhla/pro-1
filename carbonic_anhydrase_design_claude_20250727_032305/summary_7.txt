# Carbonic Anhydrase Variant Design Summary

## Main Objective
Design stable carbonic anhydrase (CA) variants with enhanced thermostability while preserving catalytic function.

## Reference Structure Analysis
- Wild-type human CA II (260 residues)
- Rosetta score: -304.41 to -305.75 REU
- Secondary structure: 17.3% helix, 26.5% sheet, 56.2% loop
- Catalytic residues: Y7, N62, H64, N67, Q92 (proton transfer network)
- Zinc-binding residues: H94, H96, H119

## Variants Designed and Tested

### 1. Disulfide Bond Variant (A23C/L203C/C206S)
- Mutations introduced disulfide bond while preventing unwanted disulfide formation
- Rosetta score: -296.62 REU
- Catalytic site RMSD: 0.089 Å
- Secondary structure maintained

### 2. Salt Bridge Variant (A245E/Q249R/Q253E)
- Enhanced salt bridge network in C-terminal region
- Rosetta score: -310.91 REU initially; -283.82 REU on re-evaluation
- Catalytic site RMSD: 0.027 Å
- Zinc binding RMSD: 0.012 Å
- Total SASA: 12,156 Å²

### 3. Combined Variant (A23C/L203C/C206S + A245E/Q249R/Q253E)
- Integrated disulfide bond and salt bridge enhancements
- Rosetta score: -300.57 REU
- Catalytic site RMSD: 0.097 Å

### 4. Hydrophobic-to-Polar Surface Variant (Salt bridge variant + V121D/V135Q/I146N)
- Added surface polar residues to the salt bridge variant
- Rosetta score: -256.60 REU
- Catalytic site RMSD: 0.037 Å
- Zinc binding RMSD: 0.003 Å
- Total SASA: 12,147 Å²

### 5. Proline Stabilization Variant (Salt bridge variant + G44P/G140P/A174P)
- Added prolines to rigidify loop regions
- Rosetta score: -102.55 REU
- Catalytic site RMSD: 0.233 Å
- Zinc binding RMSD: 0.041 Å
- Slight increase in β-sheet content (27.3%)

### 6. β-Barrel Enhanced CA (OB-fold domain insertion at position 170-171)
- Inserted thermostable OB-fold β-barrel domain with GS linkers
- Increased size to 331 residues
- Rosetta score: 445.38 REU (unfavorable)
- Catalytic site RMSD: 0.180 Å
- Zinc binding RMSD: 0.066 Å
- Radius of gyration increased to 20.93 Å

## Key Findings
- Salt bridge variant showed the best Rosetta score and well-preserved catalytic geometry
- Domain insertion approaches require refinement to improve stability
- Proline substitutions maintained catalytic geometry but didn't improve stability score
- The β-barrel domain insertion preserved catalytic function but needs optimization

## Next Design Directions
- Refine the salt bridge network approach
- Consider more carefully designed domain insertions with optimized linkers
- Explore alternative insertion sites for stabilizing domains
- Consider additional strategies such as circular permutation with stable new termini
