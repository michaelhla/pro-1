## Key Findings & Insights

1. **Most Successful Designs**:
   - CA-StrategicDisulfide: Best energy score (-259.35 REU) with excellent structural preservation
   - C-terminal zinc finger: Excellent structural preservation (2.81Å RMSD)
   - CA-EFhand-Disulfide: Excellent preservation (1.314Å) with added calcium-binding function

2. **Critical Design Principles**:
   - Proper linker design is essential for domain insertions
   - Terminal modifications are less disruptive than mid-sequence insertions
   - Combining multiple stabilization strategies (e.g., disulfide bonds + metal binding) yields synergistic benefits
   - Strategic positioning of new elements preserves catalytic integrity

3. **Promising Approaches**:
   - Strategic disulfide bonds provide stability with minimal structural disruption
   - Metal-binding motifs (zinc, calcium) offer stability through coordination chemistry
   - Domain duplication shows potential for enhancing stability
