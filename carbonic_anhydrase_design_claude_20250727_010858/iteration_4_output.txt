**Domain Insertion Strategies**

- **Randomized Insertion Libraries:** Techniques like *saturated programmable insertion engineering (SPINE)* generate comprehensive libraries by inserting a domain at virtually every possible site in the acceptor protein[^2][2]. **Loop Insertion Strategies & Stability Considerations**

- **Insertion into Surface Loops:** Short peptide or domain insertions are often most successful in *surface-exposed, flexible loops* well-distanced from active or functional sites. - **Engineering Stable Junctions:** The stability of loop insertions is improved by ensuring the inserted sequence has *compatible secondary structure propensity* or can be accommodated without introducing strain. **Deletion Strategies**
