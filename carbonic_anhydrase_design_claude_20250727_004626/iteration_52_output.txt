# Design Iteration Summary and Recommendations

Based on our design iterations and analysis, here's a summary of our findings:

## Design Results

1. **Design v6 (A134T, C205S, V207L)**
   - Rosetta Score: -253.05 REU
   - Catalytic Integrity: Excellent (RMSD: 0.053Å)
   - Secondary Structure: Well maintained (17.3% helix, 26.5% sheet)
   - Assessment: Most stable design so far

2. **Design v8 (A134T, C205S, V207L + N130D, S217K, V121E)**
   - Rosetta Score: -55.45 REU
   - Catalytic Integrity: Moderate concern (zinc binding RMSD: 1.53Å)
   - Assessment: Multiple surface charge modifications destabilized the structure

3. **Design v9 (A134T, C205S, V207L + A259T)**
   - Rosetta Score: -245.08 REU
   - Catalytic Integrity: Excellent (RMSD: 0.056Å)
   - Assessment: Single conservative mutation maintained stability

4. **Design v10 (A134T, C205S, V207L + K45T, A235P)**
   - Rosetta Score: -143.99 REU
   - Catalytic Integrity: Excellent (RMSD: 0.070Å)
   - Assessment: Additional mutations somewhat reduced stability

## Key Insights

1. **Conservative Surface Mutations Are Preferred**: Our most stable design (v6) uses conservative mutations that don't drastically alter the electrostatic properties of the protein.

2. **Catalytic Integrity**: All designs except v8 maintained excellent catalytic site geometry, which is crucial for function.

3. **Charge Alterations**: Multiple charge modifications (as in v8) significantly destabilized the structure, indicating that extensive surface charge engineering requires more careful analysis.

4. **Proline Insertions**: The proline insertion in v10 (A235P) didn't improve stability, possibly due to disruption of local interactions.

## Recommended Design

**Design v6 (A134T, C205S, V207L)** remains our most promising design with:
- Good Rosetta score (-253.05 REU)
- Excellent catalytic site preservation
- Maintained secondary structure
- Minimal RMSD from wild-type (0.216Å)

## Proposed Next Steps

1. **Experimental Validation**: Test Design v6 experimentally for thermal stability and catalytic activity.

2. **Refined Surface Engineering**: Introduce more targeted single mutations to Design v6, focusing on:
   - Surface hydrophobic-to-polar conversions at positions not critical for structure
   - Strategic salt bridge formation with careful computational analysis beforehand
   - Selective rigidification of flexible loops with proline substitutions

3. **Combined Approach**: Consider combining the known thermostable sextuple mutant (A65T, L100H, K154N, L224S, L240P, A248T) with elements of Design v6 in a carefully phased manner.

4. **Structural Analysis**: Perform molecular dynamics simulations at elevated temperatures to identify regions with high flexibility that could benefit from stabilizing mutations.

This iterative design process has yielded valuable insights into hCA II engineering strategies. The conservative Design v6 shows the most promise for further development toward a thermostable variant for carbon capture applications.
