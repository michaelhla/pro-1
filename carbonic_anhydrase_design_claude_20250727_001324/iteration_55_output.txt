**Key Mutations for Enhanced Stability/Thermostability in HCAII:**

- **Six-mutation variant (HCAIIts):**  
  This thermostable variant includes the mutations **A65T, L100H, K154N, L224S, L240P, and A248T**. The sextuple mutant retains more than 85% activity after 2 hours at 65°C, whereas the wild-type loses most activity under these conditions. - **Disulfide bond engineering (dsHCAII):**
